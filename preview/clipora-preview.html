Questions

Is my file uploaded right now?

No. Today the site only checks the file in your browser. Real uploads and processing start in Milestone 2, and this page will say so when they do.

Which formats can I use?

MP4, MOV, MP3, WAV, M4A, up to 500 MB. Each format is confirmed against the real processing pipeline before it is promised.

Will transcripts be perfectly accurate?

No. Speech-to-text can make mistakes, especially with background noise, accents, overlapping speakers and technical terms. Always review important transcripts.

Can I use a link from another video site?

No. Clipora AI only works on files you upload and are authorized to process. It does not download content from third-party platforms.

How are my files and transcripts handled?

The plan is private storage, owner-only access and deletion on request. The privacy policy will describe retention and third-party AI processing in detail before public launch.
